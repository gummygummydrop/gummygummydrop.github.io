Health Communication · Emotion · Technology

Rui Gu

I study how people turn challenging messages into meaningful change.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Communication Studies at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My research examines how emotions, morality, and technology shape the way people process health and risk messages, form beliefs, and make behavioral decisions.
