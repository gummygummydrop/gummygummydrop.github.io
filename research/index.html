Research

When messages ask something difficult of us

My overarching goal is to understand when health and risk messages that feel emotionally challenging, counterattitudinal, or identity-threatening can support reflection, informed decisions, attitude change, and healthier action.

01

Research program

Meaningful media

How can uncomfortable media become a resource for reflection rather than resistance?

I study how audiences engage with morally challenging and emotionally demanding media, including stories about illness, mortality, and care. This work includes designing and validating a scale that measures self-reflection as an inward-oriented mode of processing. I also test how narrative order and plot explicitness shape persuasion through distinct psychological pathways, and examine online mourning as a media-enabled coping system.

  • Self-reflection scale
  • Narrative persuasion
  • Health narratives
  • Online mourning
02

Research program

Dynamic emotions

What can the shape of an emotion tell us that its average intensity cannot?

I treat emotion as a process that unfolds throughout message exposure. My research models the inverted-U trajectory of fear and asks how its peakedness influences smoking cessation intentions across genders. In related work, I use mixed emotional appeals to examine how dengue narratives produce changing patterns of hope and fear, and how coactivation, dominance, and direction matter for health outcomes.

  • Emotion trajectories
  • Fear appeals
  • Mixed emotions
  • Health behavior
03

Research program

Technology & AI

How do people build trust and form health beliefs when the communicator is partly or entirely artificial?

I examine how health chatbots and AI-enabled communicators shape risk beliefs, trust, social presence, positive emotion, and willingness to self-disclose. This health communication focus connects with my broader work on technology as a stage for self-presentation and social judgment, including influencer bragging strategies and expectancy violations around VTubers and virtual influencers.

  • Health chatbots
  • Human-AI interaction
  • Trust and disclosure
  • Virtual influencers

Research collaboration

Games that make room for serious experience

Outside academia, I am an active gamer drawn to souls-like and story-rich games. I am especially interested in collaborations on how meaningful games, such as Spiritfarer, help players process mortality, illness, and care.
